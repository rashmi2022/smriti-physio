FAQ

Good questions.

What happens in the first consultation?

A detailed chat about your pain and history, a movement and strength assessment, and a clear explanation of what's going on. You'll leave with home exercises and a recommendation on whether you need therapy sessions.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends entirely on your condition and how you respond. Some people need none beyond the consultation, some need a few, and post-surgery rehab takes longer. You'll always know the plan and the fee before starting.

What should I bring or wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes that let you move and show the painful area (shorts for knee problems help). Bring any X-ray, MRI or doctor's reports you have.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No, you can book directly. If anything in the assessment suggests you need a scan or a specialist's opinion, you'll be told and referred.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Send a WhatsApp message or call. A few hours' notice helps someone else get the slot.
